Privacy Policy for Shri Kalki Puranam Premium

Effective Date: October 16, 2026

Application: श्रीकल्किपुराणम् - Kalki Purana (com.ramabhadra.shrikalkipuranam)

Key Summary: Shri Kalki Puranam Premium is an educational, sacred Vedic scripture and cultural reference application designed to be completely offline. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal information or device data to external servers or third parties.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No personal identification data (name, email, phone number, address) is collected.
  • No location tracking or GPS data is accessed.
  • No device identifiers, advertising IDs, or telemetry data are collected.
  • No financial, payment, or biometric information is processed.

2. Device Permissions and Their Purpose

The application requests only minimal Android permissions required for offline functionality:

  • Audio Recording (RECORD_AUDIO): Used exclusively for the optional, voice-activated 108-bead Japa Mala counter. When enabled by the user, the microphone detects audio volume/amplitude locally on your device to increment the counter. Audio is processed strictly in real-time in temporary device memory and is never recorded, saved, transcribed, or transmitted.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to deliver local daily spiritual quotes or shloka notifications if scheduled by the user. All alarms and notifications are managed locally on the device.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Provides subtle haptic feedback when turning beads during Japa meditation.

3. Local Data Storage

Any bookmarks, reading progress notes, or app preferences (e.g., chosen theme, font size, sound pitch) are stored exclusively in your device's private local SQLite database. You can clear this data at any time via App Settings → Reset Data, or by uninstalling the app.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Shri Kalki Puranam Premium contains zero third-party advertisements (Ad-Free), zero analytics trackers, and zero external software development kits (SDKs) that monitor user behavior.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is suitable for general audiences of all ages interested in spiritual literature. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Theological and Cultural Disclaimer

This application is dedicated exclusively to the study and preservation of the sacred Sanskrit text of the Kalki Purana and its authentic Nepali commentary. It promotes ethical virtues, righteousness (Dharma), and spiritual wisdom, without malice toward any caste, creed, community, nationality, or faith.
