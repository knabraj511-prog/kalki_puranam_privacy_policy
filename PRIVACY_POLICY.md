## 2. Device Permissions and Usage
The app requests minimal Android permissions solely to power local, device-only features:
1. **Microphone (`android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`):**
   - **Purpose:** Used strictly for the voice-activated 108-bead Japa Mala counter when enabled by the user.
   - **Privacy Protection:** Audio amplitude is evaluated entirely in-memory on the device in real-time. No voice or audio data is ever recorded, transcribed, stored, or transmitted over any network.
2. **Notifications (`android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):**
   - **Purpose:** Delivers local daily spiritual quotes or shloka reminders if configured by the user.
   - **Privacy Protection:** Managed entirely by local device alarms.
3. **Vibration (`android.permission.VIBRATE`):**
   - **Purpose:** Provides subtle haptic feedback when turning beads during Japa meditation.
